# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Instagram visitors arriving from Evan's bio and recruiters assessing creative-development talent. Visitors need quick proof of projects, education, personal character, and hands-on technical work without feeling sold to.

## Product Purpose

Present Evan Octave's portfolio as a memorable, interactive record of projects and practice. Success means a visitor can understand his strengths, inspect real work, and leave with a clear sense of his personality and technical range.

## Brand Personality

Playful, tactile, self-assured. Spherical objects, dimensional movement, and quiet black-and-white contrast create a portfolio that feels alive without becoming corporate or desperate.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic recruiter-bait, sterile SaaS layouts, conventional professional headshots, flat card grids, and passive scrolling. Avoid frantic interaction that obscures reading or blocks project access.

## Design Principles

1. Show evidence, not self-promotion.
2. Make exploration rewarding, never required.
3. Let physical making show through digital form.
4. Keep projects easy to inspect while personality stays unmistakable.
5. Use motion as an invitation, not a distraction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard-only exploration, clear visible focus, 44px touch targets, readable contrast, semantic landmarks, and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Interactive decoration must never hide or gate core portfolio content.
